# AYOJA — Demo Video Script
### HackToSkill PromptWars | Challenge 5: Celebration & Event Planner

**Scene 1 — Introduction (0:00–0:15)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
Open https://eventplanner2.vercel.app. Show the AYOJA homepage and logo. Keep the screen still for a moment.

**SPEAK:**  
"Hello everyone! I'm Neel, and this is AYOJA — an AI-powered Indian celebration and event planning platform.

Planning an event involves much more than choosing a venue. It requires managing budgets, vendors, timelines, traditions and people. AYOJA brings all of that together in one place."

---

**Scene 2 — Creating an Event Plan (0:15–0:50)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
1. Click **Start Planning My Event**.  
2. Select **Wedding**.  
3. Enter *Aarav & Diya's Gujarati Wedding*.  
4. Set the event date to **25 February 2027**.  
5. Select the wedding ceremonies.  
6. Click **Continue**.  
7. Enter **350 guests**, **Bhuj, Gujarat** and **₹15,00,000**.  
8. Select **Banquet Hall** and continue.  
9. Select **Traditional** and **Royal** preferences. Enter **Jain** and **Pure Vegetarian** requirements.  
10. Click **Generate Event Plan**.  

**SPEAK:**  
"Let's take an example of a Gujarati wedding in Bhuj, with 350 guests and a budget of 15 lakh rupees.

The user can specify the event type, date, location, guest count, budget, cultural traditions and personal preferences.

AYOJA uses this information to create a structured event plan, tailored to the occasion and its requirements."

---

**Scene 3 — Intelligent Budget Management (0:50–1:12)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
1. Show the **Budget** tab.  
2. Point to the donut chart and budget categories.  
3. Move one category's percentage slider slightly.  
4. Show the other allocations adjusting.  

**SPEAK:**  
"Budget management is one of AYOJA's key features.

The platform distributes the total budget across different categories and provides an interactive financial breakdown.

Users can adjust individual allocations, while the remaining categories automatically rebalance. Our calculation engine ensures that the percentages add up to 100 percent and the allocated amounts match the exact total budget."

---

**Scene 4 — Timeline and Vendor Management (1:12–1:43)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
1. Open **Timeline**.  
2. Show the event countdown and task completion progress.  
3. Point out a venue prerequisite and a dependent task.  
4. Open **Vendors**.  
5. Click **Compare 3 Quotes & Audit Hidden Costs** on one vendor card.  
6. Show the three quotation tiers.  

**SPEAK:**  
"Next, AYOJA helps organise the work that needs to happen before the event.

The timeline automatically adjusts deadlines according to the event date and highlights important prerequisites, such as securing a venue before proceeding with dependent arrangements.

The vendor dashboard tracks estimated and actual expenses, booking status and payment milestones.

Users can also compare indicative quotation packages and review potential hidden costs before making decisions."

---

**Scene 5 — What-If Scenario Simulator (1:43–2:05)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
1. Return to the dashboard.  
2. Click **Trade-Offs & 20% Spike**.  
3. Select the 20% cost inflation scenario.  
4. Show the Food First strategy and the revised allocation.  
5. Briefly show the other available scenarios.  

**SPEAK:**  
"But what happens when real life doesn't follow the original plan?

AYOJA includes a scenario and trade-off simulator. Users can explore unexpected cost increases, additional guests, reduced budgets and accelerated timelines.

They can evaluate different priorities and see how the budget distribution changes before applying a revised plan."

---

**Scene 6 — AI-Powered Assistance (2:05–2:25)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
1. Open the floating **Event Bestie** assistant.  
2. Enter: *'Suggest three ways to make this Gujarati wedding more cost-effective while preserving its traditional feel.'*  
3. Show the response.  

**SPEAK:**  
"AYOJA also integrates generative AI through Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini.

The AI powers event-plan generation, creative idea regeneration and a conversational planning assistant.

Users can ask questions, explore alternatives and get personalised suggestions instead of starting their planning process from scratch."

---

**Scene 7 — Event-Day Operations and Sharing (2:25–2:48)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
1. Open **Operations**.  
2. Show the hour-by-hour event run-of-show.  
3. Preview the optional digital VIP guest pass.  
4. Open **WhatsApp RSVP & Passes**.  
5. Show the invite preview.  
6. Open **One-Click Editorial PDF** and show the print-ready handout.  

**SPEAK:**  
"Finally, AYOJA takes planning beyond preparation and into event-day coordination.

The Operations dashboard provides an hour-by-hour run-of-show with assigned coordinators and task tracking.

It also provides digital guest-pass previews, WhatsApp invitation tools and printable event handouts.

This helps families and event organisers keep important information organised and accessible."

---

**Scene 8 — Closing (2:48–3:00)**

**ON SCREEN:**  
Return to the AYOJA logo or homepage. Hold the final screen for a few seconds.

**SPEAK:**  
"From the first idea to the final celebration, AYOJA brings planning, creativity and coordination together.

AYOJA — Your Plans. Your People. Your Moments.

Thank you!"
